Yelp is an online platform that helps people discover local businesses through business information, photos, ratings, and customer reviews. Founded in San Francisco in 2004, Yelp operates Yelp.com and mobile apps and is publicly traded on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol **YELP**. [Yelp company facts](https://www.yelp-press.com/company/fast-facts/default.aspx)

## What consumers can do

People can search for businesses and services, compare business details and reviews, and contact businesses. Depending on the business and location, Yelp supports actions such as requesting a quote, booking a reservation or appointment, joining a restaurant waitlist, and ordering food. The available actions vary by listing and market. [Yelp company facts](https://www.yelp-press.com/company/fast-facts/default.aspx)

Yelp has also introduced AI-supported discovery features. Its company updates describe Yelp Assistant, natural-language search, and features that help people explore business information and complete tasks such as reservations or ordering. Product availability and integrations can change. [Yelp product updates](https://www.yelp-press.com/press-releases)

## Tools for businesses

Businesses can claim and manage Yelp pages, maintain details such as hours, respond to reviews, and use advertising products. Yelp offers advertising and tools for customer inquiries and transactions; the available features and costs depend on the product and business. [Yelp for Business](https://business.yelp.com/)

Yelp's role differs from Hibu's. Yelp is primarily a consumer discovery and transaction platform that also sells business tools and advertising. Hibu is primarily a marketing services provider for businesses. A local business may use both.

## Company and platform snapshot

Yelp reported **330 million cumulative reviews as of December 31, 2025**. Its company facts page says the total includes reviews that are recommended, not recommended, or removed. [Yelp company facts](https://www.yelp-press.com/company/fast-facts/default.aspx)

In its November 5, 2026 third-quarter results, Yelp reported quarterly net revenue of **$371 million** and updated its full-year 2026 net revenue outlook to **$1.455 billion–$1.460 billion**. The revenue range is a company forecast for the full year, not a completed annual result. [Yelp Q3 2026 results](https://www.yelp-press.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2026/Yelp-Reports-Third-Quarter-2026-Results/default.aspx)

These figures are dated snapshots. For newer numbers, check Yelp's [company facts](https://www.yelp-press.com/company/fast-facts/default.aspx) and [press releases](https://www.yelp-press.com/press-releases) before updating this page.

## How Yelp fits into local search

Local discovery often combines search, maps, directories, business websites, reviews, and direct booking or messaging. Yelp is one destination in that ecosystem. It also appears as a publisher in Yext's FY2026 publisher network, illustrating that a consumer platform can also serve as a channel through which business information is distributed. [Yext FY2026 annual report](https://investors.yext.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001628280-26-016402/yext-20260131.htm)

## Other meaning

Outside the company name, **yelp** is an English word for a short, sharp cry or bark, often expressing pain, alarm, or excitement. [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/yelp)
