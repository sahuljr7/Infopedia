**Hibu**, it is a U.S.-focused **digital marketing company for local and small businesses**. It has an interesting history because it evolved from the traditional **Yellow Pages / directory business** into a modern digital-marketing provider.

## 1. What exactly does Hibu do?

In simple terms:

> **Hibu helps local businesses get found online and turn online searches into customers.**

Its customers are generally businesses such as:

- Home-service companies
- Lawyers
- Healthcare providers
- Automotive businesses
- Contractors
- Restaurants and local retailers
- Other small and medium-sized businesses
- Multi-location businesses

Hibu currently markets an integrated platform called **Hibu One**, which combines multiple digital-marketing activities into one system. [Hibu](https://hibu.com/why-hibu/about-us?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### Main services

| Service | What Hibu does |
|---|---|
| **Website development** | Builds websites for local businesses |
| **SEO** | Helps businesses appear in Google/search results |
| **AI search optimization** | Helps businesses become visible in AI-driven search |
| **Paid advertising** | Runs Google, Facebook, Amazon, Bing and other advertising campaigns |
| **Listings management** | Keeps business information accurate across online directories |
| **Review generation** | Helps businesses collect customer reviews |
| **Reputation management** | Monitors/responds to online reviews and mentions |
| **Email/SMS marketing** | Helps businesses communicate with existing leads/customers |
| **Lead management** | Tracks calls, texts and other leads |
| **Analytics** | Shows businesses where leads and customers are coming from |

Hibu says its platform uses AI and predictive targeting to optimize campaigns and uses its proprietary local-business data. [Hibu](https://hibu.com/why-hibu/about-us?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

# 2. Think of Hibu like this

Imagine you own a plumbing company in Chicago.

Someone searches:

Hibu can potentially help your business appear through:

**Google Search → Paid Ads → Website → Phone Call/Form → Lead Tracking → Follow-up SMS/Email → Customer**

**Digital agency + advertising company + SEO company + website provider + local-search platform + lead-management system**

# 3. Hibu's history

The history is actually quite long.

### 1930 — The beginning

The roots of the U.S. business go back to **Merchant Advertising Services**, established in Long Island in 1930.

It eventually became **Yellow Book USA**. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hibu?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### 1996 — Yellow Pages moves online

In the UK, British Telecommunications launched **Yell.com**, an online local-search service.

### 1999 — BT acquires Yellow Book USA

British Telecommunications acquired Yellow Book USA for approximately **$665 million**.

This brought the U.S. Yellow Pages operation into the broader Yell organization. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hibu?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### 2000 — Yell

The combined group became known as **Yell**.

It expanded significantly through acquisitions.

### 2002 — McLeodUSA

Yell acquired McLeodUSA's directory business for approximately $600 million.

### 2003 — Public company

Yell was listed on the London Stock Exchange.

### 2006–2007 — Spain

Yell expanded into Spain by acquiring a majority stake in Telefónica's directory business, eventually taking control of the company. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hibu?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

# 4. The big transformation: print → digital

This is probably the most important part of Hibu's story.

For decades, its core business was basically:

**Yellow Pages**

Businesses paid to appear in printed directories.

But the internet fundamentally changed that industry.

People stopped searching for businesses in physical Yellow Pages books and started using:

- Google
- Bing
- Yelp
- Facebook
- Online directories
- Mobile search

So Hibu/Yell had to transform itself.

In 2011, Yell announced a multi-year strategy to move away from traditional print and toward **digital services for small businesses**. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hibu?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

That included:

**Websites → PPC → SEO → Online advertising → Local search → Digital marketing**

---

# 5. 2012 — The name Hibu

In 2012, Yell announced that the group would change its name to **Hibu**, pronounced **"Hi-boo."**

The name officially became effective in July 2012. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hibu?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The company wanted to position itself as something more modern than a traditional Yellow Pages publisher.

---

# 6. 2014 — An interesting reversal

Here's something that can be confusing.

The **UK Hibu business later returned to the Yell brand**.

In 2014, Hibu UK changed its customer-facing brand back to **Yell**, while offering products such as:

- Websites
- PPC
- Display advertising
- Digital marketing

So today you should distinguish between:

### Hibu
Primarily the **U.S. digital marketing business**.

### Yell
The **UK business**, which retained the Yell brand.

The underlying corporate history connects the two. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yell_%28company%29?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

---

# 7. H.I.G. Capital

A major modern development occurred in **2021**.

Private-equity firm **H.I.G. Capital acquired Hibu Inc.** from Hibu Group Limited. Following the transaction, the broader group changed its name back to Yell Group Limited. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hibu?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

So the modern Hibu business is no longer simply the old publicly traded Yellow Pages company.

---

# 8. 2024 — Acquisition of RevLocal

Another important development:

In **August 2024, Hibu acquired RevLocal**, a digital marketing company focused on local and small-to-medium-sized businesses and multi-location brands. [Hibu](https://hibu.com/company/company-news?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

This is significant because RevLocal operates in essentially the same broad market.

It strengthened Hibu's position in:

**Local businesses → Digital marketing → Lead generation → Multi-location marketing**

# 11. Hibu vs Google/Yelp

One important distinction:

**Google and Yelp aren't exactly the same type of competitor.**

Google and Yelp primarily provide consumer discovery/search/review platforms.

Hibu is primarily a **service provider/marketing technology partner for the business itself**.

For example:

**Consumer**

> "Best dentist near me"

↓

Google

↓

Dentist's website/reviews

Meanwhile:

**Dentist**

↓

Hibu

↓

Website + SEO + Google Ads + listings + reviews + lead tracking

So Hibu can actually help a business market itself **on Google and other platforms**.

Hibu says its advertising platform can target customers through Google, Facebook, Amazon, Bing and other channels. [Hibu](https://hibu.com/campaign/paid-home?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

# 12. Where Hibu makes money

The basic business model is **B2B recurring digital marketing services**.

A local business pays Hibu for some combination of:

**Website + SEO + advertising + listings + reputation + marketing automation + lead generation**

The exact pricing depends on the package and business.

This is different from a pure SaaS model where the customer simply pays $20/month for software.

Hibu combines:

**Software + managed services + advertising + human expertise**

That's an important characteristic of the company.

# 13. Why the Yellow Pages history matters

Hibu's biggest historical advantage is arguably its **long history with local businesses and local-business data**.

The company didn't start as a modern SaaS startup.

It came from the directory industry.

The evolution is roughly:

**1930s**

Yellow Pages / directories

↓

**1990s**

Online directories

↓

**2000s**

Internet advertising

↓

**2010s**

Websites + PPC + digital marketing

↓

**2020s**

AI + SEO + omnichannel advertising + automation

↓

**Today**

**Hibu One**

That makes Hibu an interesting example of an old directory business transforming into a technology-enabled digital marketing company.

Hibu itself emphasizes its decades of local-business data and campaign-management experience as part of Hibu One. [Hibu](https://hibu.com/why-hibu/about-us?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
