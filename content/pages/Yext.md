Yext is a software company whose platform helps organizations manage information about their brands, locations, services, and professionals across websites and other digital channels. Its roots are in local listings; its current platform also includes products for reviews, pages, search, social publishing, customer relationships, and brand visibility. [Yext company information](https://investors.yext.com/company-information)

## How the platform works

At the center of Yext's platform is the **Knowledge Graph**, a structured store for facts such as a location's name, address, phone number, hours, services, and frequently asked questions. Organizations can manage this information centrally and use it to power customer-facing pages or distribute it to third-party services.

Yext's fiscal 2026 annual report says its publisher network included more than 200 service and application providers, including Apple, Bing, Facebook, Gemini, Google, OpenAI, and Yelp. That figure and list describe the network reported for the fiscal year ended January 31, 2026. [Yext FY2026 annual report](https://investors.yext.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001628280-26-016402/yext-20260131.htm)

For a multi-location organization, a central source of structured data can make it easier to update shared details across multiple channels. The organization still needs to keep its source information accurate and confirm which publishers, fields, and features are supported for its account.

Yext describes these as products of its digital presence platform, with analytics capabilities. Names and packaging can change; consult the current [Yext platform overview](https://www.yext.com/platform) for current product details.

## Search, AI, and agents

Search engine optimization traditionally focuses on helping pages appear in search results. As people also use AI assistants to ask questions, organizations have an additional reason to keep brand information structured, accurate, and available to the services that may use it. Structured data can help systems retrieve details about a business, but it does not guarantee that a particular AI system will mention or recommend it.

In June 2026, Yext described an agentic marketing platform built on its Knowledge Graph, Scout visibility data, and publisher network. The company said teams could use the platform through its interface, APIs, or Model Context Protocol (MCP). These are company-described capabilities; access and available actions depend on the product and configuration. [Yext agentic marketing announcement](https://investors.yext.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/388/yext-opens-full-platform-for-trusted-agentic-marketing)

The concepts behind this approach include:

- **Structured data and knowledge graphs:** Representing entities such as businesses, locations, services, and the relationships among them.
- **Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG):** A general AI pattern in which a system retrieves relevant information before generating a response. It is a useful way to understand possible applications of structured business data, not a claim that every Yext feature uses RAG.
- **Agents and APIs:** Software can use defined tools or interfaces to retrieve information and perform permitted actions.
- **MCP:** A protocol that can connect AI applications to external tools and data sources. Yext has announced MCP access as one way to interact with its platform.
- **Analytics:** Measures activity or visibility across supported customer experiences; results depend on the channels and measurements available.

For example, an authorized workflow might identify locations with missing hours and help an employee update holiday hours. Any real update should follow the organization's review and access controls.

## Where Yext fits

Yext serves organizations that need to manage business information across many locations and digital touchpoints. Companies such as Birdeye, BrightLocal, Semrush, Moz, and Rio SEO operate in overlapping areas such as reputation management, local SEO, listings, or search visibility. Their products and customer focus differ, so they are examples of adjacent competitors rather than direct equivalents in every use case.

Yext was founded in 2006 and is listed on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol **YEXT**. [Yext company information](https://investors.yext.com/company-information)

## Summary

Yext's central idea is **structured brand information plus distribution and digital-presence tools**. Its product scope extends beyond listings into reviews, pages, search, social, analytics, and AI-oriented visibility and workflows. The practical value depends on the quality of the information an organization maintains, its chosen products, and the channels it needs to support.
